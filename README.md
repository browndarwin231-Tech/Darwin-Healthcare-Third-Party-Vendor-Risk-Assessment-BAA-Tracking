# Healthcare Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment & BAA Tracking

**Organization:** Nova Care Cloud (fictional)  
**Role:** GRC Analyst (portfolio simulation)  
**Focus:** HIPAA-aligned vendor due diligence, PHI access scoping, business associate agreement (BAA) tracking, risk escalation, and evidence management.

## Scenario
Nova Care Cloud is onboarding and reviewing six fictional providers. You are tasked with identifying which vendors may process protected health information (PHI), checking BAA status, requesting security assurances, scoring identified risks, and preparing a decision summary for the CISO.

## Deliverables
- `Nova_Care_Cloud_Vendor_Risk_BAA_Tracker.xlsx`: Dashboard, vendor inventory, risk assessments, BAA register, evidence tracking, findings and references.
- `VENDOR_RISK_ASSESSMENT_PLAN.md`: assessment approach and escalation thresholds.
- `CISO_RISK_SUMMARY.md`: fictional assessment outcomes and next steps.
- `ANALYST_EXERCISES.md`: analyst review exercises.
- `sample_evidence/`: mock questionnaire and evidence excerpts.

## How to practice
1. Open the workbook and review the **Vendor Inventory** tab.
2. Compare vendors' PHI exposure with their **BAA Register** status.
3. Review the sample evidence and determine which vendors should be approved, conditionally approved, or blocked.
4. Verify the open findings and proposed action dates in **Findings & Actions**.
5. Write a short decision memo for the CISO, citing the evidence IDs.

**Important:** All vendors, documentation and review results are fictional. This is a training simulation, not a real audit, legal determination, or HITRUST certification. Do not include actual patient records in a public GitHub repo.
